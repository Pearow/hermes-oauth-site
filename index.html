Hermes Personal Assistant

A personal, single-user assistant operated by Pearow for organizing tasks, notes, and calendar activities. It is not a public sign-up service.

Google Calendar integration

With the owner's authorization, the assistant lists calendars, reads events and availability, and creates, edits, or deletes individual events in selected existing calendars when instructed. It does not request Gmail access.

The assistant is configured to use a locally hosted AI model. Calendar information may appear in replies delivered to the owner through Telegram. This website is informational: it does not connect to Google Calendar, collect credentials, or host the assistant.
