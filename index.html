Hermes Personal Assistant

A private personal assistant for its owner, helping with requested email and calendar information, reminders and briefings.

When enabled, cloud AI processing through Cloudflare or Anthropic may receive relevant requests, conversation context and tool results. Google and Telegram also process information for their services. AI processing is not guaranteed to be local. See the Privacy Policy.

What it can do

  • Owner-requested calendar assistance, with preview and later confirmation for edits or deletion.
  • On-demand email search and summaries, explicitly requested attachments and narrowly confirmed mark-as-read actions.
  • On-demand briefings, without background email monitoring.

Supported features, Google permissions and data-handling limits are described in the Privacy Policy.

Contact — issues are public. Never post private information or credentials.
