Privacy Policy

A private assistant for its owner. Updated October 23, 2026.

Purpose and limits

  • Calendar: selected-calendar reads and explicitly requested individual timed events. Edits or deletion require a full preview and later confirmation. No attendees, invitations, bulk or whole-series changes.
  • Gmail: on-demand search/read, specifically requested attachment downloads, and separately previewed/later-confirmed mark-as-read for eligible routine promotions/newsletters. No sending, replying, drafting, archiving, deletion, trashing or other label changes. Attachments are not automatically downloaded, opened, extracted or executed.
  • Briefings: up to five email highlights on request. No background email monitoring or bulk mailbox export.

Google access is authorized by the owner. Grants may permit more actions than the assistant exposes; workflow limits are not credential-level restrictions.

Processing and sharing

When enabled, Cloudflare Workers AI may process relevant requests, history and tool results, including requested email/attachments, calendar data, notes, files and voice transcripts. Raw voice recordings are not sent for cloud transcription. Private content is not used as web-search material. Google and Telegram process data for their respective services. AI processing is not guaranteed to be local.

The assistant does not sell data, serve ads or train models on private content. Cloudflare states that Workers AI does not train on customer content and normally does not retain stateless inputs/outputs after processing, subject to safety, security and legal exceptions. Operational metadata may remain. See Cloudflare's data-usage policy.

Retention and owner control

The owner can revoke Google access and remove retained assistant records. Revocation does not automatically erase stored history, credentials, files or backups. Removing assistant copies does not delete Gmail messages or provider records. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

Website information

This static GitHub Pages site has no forms, scripts, analytics, cookies or external assets. Hosting request data is subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Google policies

Google API data use and transfer will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use, and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy.

Contact

Contact — issues are public. Never post private information or credentials.
